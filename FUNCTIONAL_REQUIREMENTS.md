# Functional requirements

- One owner unlocks the room with a numeric PIN, without email sign-in. Before unlocking, the screen shows only "Welcome. Let's bloom." and the PIN field.
- A status (focus, break, eating, away, done) is saved with the timer.
- A Pomodoro offers 25/5, 50/10 and custom durations, with pause, resume, break and end controls. Focus completion starts a break automatically.
- Focus time excludes pauses and is clipped to the local day.
- Connection failures are shown and state reloads after reconnection.
